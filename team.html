Meet Our Team

Our dedicated team at Dr. Dana's Practice is committed to providing professional, friendly and patient-focused dental care. Each member of our team plays an important role in creating a comfortable and welcoming experience for every patient.

Dr. Dana - Dentist

Dr. Dana is a young and passionate female dentist and co-founder of Dr. Dana's Practice. She studied at the University of Western Cape (UWC), where she completed her Bachelor of Dental Surgery (BDS) qualification. She is an ambitious and inspiring woman who is dedicated to using her knowledge and skills to provide professional, patient-focused dental care. She aims to help patients feel confident about their smiles and receive quality dental care at every stage of their treatment.

Receptionist

Dental Assistant

Our dental assistant supports the dentist during procedures and helps ensure that every patient receives excellent care.
