Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to some of the most frequently asked questions about our dental practice and services.

What services does the practice offer?

Dr Dana's Practice offers a wide range of dental services including general dentistry, teeth cleaning, teeth whitening, dental check-ups and dental consultations.

How can I make an appointment?

You can make an appointment by calling our phone number or filling out the enquiry form on our website.

What are the practice's trading hours?

Our trading hours are Monday to Friday from 8:00 AM to 17:00 PM, and Saturday from 9:00 AM to 13:00 PM. We are closed on Sundays.

What is the practice's cancellation policy?

Please contact us at least 24 hours in advance to cancel or reschedule your appointment.

Do you accept all medical aids?

Yes, we accept all major medical aids.
